### [🚐 MostlyLevel](https://github.com/roman-mueller-ch/MostlyLevel)

**A touchscreen levelling display for campervans.** An ESP32-S3 display reads a Bluetooth tilt sensor in the van and shows live how the van is tilted and how many centimetres to lift each wheel to get it level. Aircraft-style horizon view, wedge advice, four languages, plus an Android app that does the same on a phone.

`MicroPython` `ESP32-S3` `Bluetooth LE` `Android`

### [📸 Shelfie](https://github.com/roman-mueller-ch/shelfie-parts)

**Snap a part. Know what you have.** A self-hosted inventory for electronic parts: point your phone at a part, its bag or an AliExpress listing, and an AI vision model identifies it, checks whether you already own it, files it in Part-DB and prints a QR label. No more waiting three weeks for a part that was already in Box 4.

`ASP.NET Core` `React PWA` `Azure OpenAI` `Part-DB` `Tailscale` `Terraform`

### [🎲 early-cube-bridge](https://github.com/roman-mueller-ch/early-cube-bridge)

**Use the EARLY (Timeular) time-tracking cube without the desktop app.** An ESP32 listens to the cube over Bluetooth LE and talks to the EARLY API over WiFi: flip the cube and the right activity starts. Nothing to install on the work laptop, and it survives WiFi and API outages.

`C++` `ESP32` `PlatformIO` `Bluetooth LE` `REST API`
